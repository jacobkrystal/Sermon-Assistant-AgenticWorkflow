## Business Components Detail

### 1. Dialysis Center (Core)
- **Origin**: Korean dialysis center model as benchmark
- **Differentiation**: Hotel-grade service + Korean facility standards
- **Key Innovation**: Service design embedded from construction phase
- **Market**: Philippine dialysis demand (growing — CKD prevalence)

### 2. Coffee Franchise (Dual Model)
- **Large**: Master franchise operation
- **Small (₱1M budget)**: In-hospital/in-dialysis-center micro-cafes
- **Strategic Role**: Patient comfort + captive market + revenue diversification

### 3. Acupuncture Clinic
- **Type**: Traditional complementary medicine (Korean/TCM)
- **Strategic Role**: Holistic patient care, pain management for dialysis patients
- **Integration**: Co-located or near dialysis center

### 4. AI Health App (Digital Health)
- **Function 1**: Patient self-diagnosis / self-triage
- **Function 2**: Telemedicine / remote consultation for dialysis patients
- **Function 3**: Health education delivery
- **Strategic Role**: Digital extension of care, patient empowerment

### 5. International Kindergarten
- **Status**: Currently operating
- **Type**: International school (kindergarten level)
- **Strategic Role**: Community anchor, education hub
- **Potential Link**: Health education for families, community trust building

### 6. Hotel Experience (Foundation)
- **Role**: Service mindset DNA — infused into all other businesses
- **Transfer Mechanism**: Direct application of hospitality standards to healthcare
